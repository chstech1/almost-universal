# November 20, 2026 development update

Changes since the November 19 GitHub update, [`2af59af`](https://github.com/chstech1/ununiversal-remote/commit/2af59af).

## Verified on the V06 spare

- Captured the stock DSP startup over receive-only UART. Used a visible LCD CHECK build to confirm custom code reached the display. Then demonstrated native Home and Controls navigation, a timed three-second splash, and dismissal by a fresh press that did not trigger a menu action.
- Used normal Bluetooth update entry for DSP-only experiments while keeping stock v4.8 ARM Bluetooth and resources. Also demonstrated passive KATA + Reset recovery entry, a normal reset back to the menu, and a separate full v4.8 stock rollback that restored the display.
- Went from a one-button marker to screen-aware Bluetooth presses with MQTT off. Repeated Controls A taps toggled a Home Assistant test switch once per tap. Watch TV A triggered a separate configured test action. Offline D returned locally and nested Back restored focus.
- Brought up the graphical Activities, Room controls, Scenes, and Watch TV screens. Fixed the RGB565 byte order after the first palette rendered incorrectly. Recovered a working icon menu after a later blank-screen trial by replacing two optional Xtensa multiply instructions.
- Tested the Office profile with Home Assistant integration 0.1.4. The office lights and fan toggled. The blind and paired curtains responded to Open, Close, and Stop. Vizio Volume +, Volume −, and Mute worked in a labeled retest. A follow-up DSP build displayed a higher Activities title and the stock battery icon. Home Assistant reconnected after the update.
- Enabled stock Raise to Wake and observed pickup wake. The splash replayed on an ordinary wake, leaving wake behavior and menu retention open.

## Built and tested offline

- Added checked stock restore to the OTA CLI, normal-mode version readback, explicit identity matching, pinned package preflight, destination and acknowledgment checks, and early rejection of unverified partial recovery commands.
- Published DSP source, build patches, and QEMU fixtures for the display, action protocol, full splash, Office controls, compact battery header, and diagnostic experiments. The splash crops reproduce all 76,800 display pixels at RGB565 precision. Three further builds cover the shared header, a visible wake diagnostic, and a dedicated Watch TV entry report.
- Added stock-ARM fixed profiles and action bindings for switches, lights, fans, covers, scripts, and TV controls. Exercised Bluetooth reconnect, duplicate handling, queued actions, and unload against simulated clients.
- Added a Home Assistant Menu Builder with a clickable preview, separate draft storage, revision checks, JSON import and export, and device layout projection. Modeled separate navigation, volume, power, and readout targets. Added an opt-in script or automation on deliberate page entry. The paired 0.1.6 Home Assistant and fixed Watch TV DSP proof passed with simulated GATT and Xtensa, respectively.
- Modeled Home Assistant state snapshots and deltas, including current temperature and units, stale readings, explicit unavailability, and fresh state after reconnect. Added display references for Climate, device status, and TV media layout. These checks use fixture input.
- Updated the teardown, UART, DSP, recovery, wire protocol, and Home Assistant documentation. Added four cropped hardware photos and kept the offline reference renders available alongside them.

## Still open

- Test the 0.1.6 integration and paired Watch TV entry DSP together on the physical remote. Verify one action on a deliberate entry, including after reconnect, and exercise the shared header on hardware.
- Diagnose the splash replay on wake, check battery refresh and sleep timing, and retest quick taps after a hold, first press after reconnect, and recovery on the compact-header build.
- Finish broader regression testing across buttons and devices. Complete independent wired ARM recovery, a second U6 read and restore test, and physical testing of the replacement ARM build. Handheld IR work remains open.
